Playset privacy policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

Playset is a free, ad-free fan app made by Nina Forge to keep track of your Star Wars: Unlimited card collection. This policy explains what the app does with your data. In short: almost nothing, because everything stays on your phone.

Who is responsible

Nina Forge. Contact: ninaforgedev@gmail.com.

What data Playset has and where it is stored

Your collection, lists, settings and achievements are stored only on your phone. Playset has no accounts and no server of its own, and it receives none of that data. Nina Forge cannot see it.

The camera and photos

Playset asks for camera permission to scan your cards. The image is processed on your phone with Google ML Kit text recognition, whose model ships inside the app. Photos are not saved and are not sent anywhere.

What does leave your phone

  • Card images. Your phone requests them from the official Star Wars: Unlimited image site (cdn.starwarsunlimited.com). As with any internet request, that server can see your IP address and device type. Playset sends nothing else and does not control what that site does with this information.
  • What you choose to share. When you save a backup, export a SWUDB CSV or share a list as text, Playset hands you a file or text and you choose where it goes (WhatsApp, Drive, email…). From there, that destination applies its own policy. If you write from “Report a problem” or “Suggest an improvement”, the email already includes the Playset and catalog versions, the phone maker and model, the Android version and the app language (for an improvement, only the Playset version). You see them before sending and can delete them.

What Playset does not do

  • It has no accounts or sign-up.
  • It shows no ads.
  • It uses no analytics, telemetry or crash reports that send data off your phone.
  • It does not sell or share data, because it does not receive any.

Google ML Kit

Text recognition uses Google's ML Kit library, which runs on the device. According to Google's own documentation, Google may collect technical usage metrics from the library: https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

How long data is kept and how to delete it

Data stays on your phone until you delete it. You can delete your collection in Settings with "Delete the collection", or delete all the app data by uninstalling it or from Android settings. Backups and files you exported are yours: Playset cannot delete them from wherever you saved them.

Children

Playset collects no data from anyone, including children. It is not specifically directed at children under 13.
